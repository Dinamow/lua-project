# SOP: Salary Certificate Issuance (Mock)

**Applies to:** All entities

1. Employee requests a salary certificate (for a bank loan, visa, or personal use) via the HR agent or HR coordinator, stating the purpose and language required (Arabic/English).
2. HR generates the certificate from BambooHR employee data (name, job title, hire date, monthly basic salary, and — if for a bank — the currency and IBAN on file).
3. Certificate is signed digitally by HR and stamped with the entity's official company stamp for embassy/bank submissions.
4. Delivered to the employee within 2 business days (same day for urgent visa-related requests, flagged as urgent).

**Note:** Salary certificates for loan purposes may require additional employer confirmation letters depending on the requesting bank — check with HR if unsure.
