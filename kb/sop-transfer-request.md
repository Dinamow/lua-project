# SOP: Internal Transfer Request (Mock)

**Applies to:** All entities (KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan)

1. Employee submits a transfer request stating the target department/location and reason, at least 30 days before the desired effective date.
2. Current line manager and receiving department manager both review and approve or reject within 5 business days.
3. HR verifies there's an open headcount slot and that the move doesn't affect Nitaqat/Saudization ratios for either entity (KSA only).
4. If approved, HR updates the employee's record in BambooHR (department, location, manager, compensation band if changed) and issues a transfer letter.
5. For cross-country transfers, HR additionally checks work-permit/visa requirements for the destination country before finalizing.

**Typical turnaround:** 10-15 business days end to end.
