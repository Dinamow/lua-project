# SOP: Housing Allowance Policy (Mock)

**Applies to:** All entities; figures below are illustrative.

- **KSA:** 25% of annual basic salary, paid monthly with payroll, unless the employee occupies company-provided housing (in which case no cash allowance is paid).
- **UAE:** 20% of annual basic salary for Dubai/Abu Dhabi-based staff, 15% for other emirates.
- **Egypt & Jordan:** Flat monthly allowance by grade band, reviewed annually by HR/Finance.

**To request a change (e.g. switching from company housing to cash allowance):**
1. Submit the request via the HR agent, stating the reason and desired effective date.
2. HR confirms current housing status in BambooHR and checks entity-specific eligibility rules.
3. Approved changes take effect from the first day of the following payroll cycle.

**Note:** Housing allowance is separate from the annual leave and gratuity calculations and does not factor into "basic salary" used for those calculations unless the employment contract states otherwise.
