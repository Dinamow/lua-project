# Labour Law & Probation Overview (Mock Knowledge Base)

This is illustrative reference material for the HR Assistant agent — simplified for demo purposes, not a substitute for legal counsel.

## Saudi Arabia (KSA)

- **Governing law:** Saudi Labour Law (Royal Decree No. M/51).
- **Probation period:** Default 90 days, extendable once to 180 days by written agreement.
- **Annual leave:** 21 days/year; rises to 30 days/year once tenure reaches 5 years (Article 109).
- **Sick leave:** 30 days full pay, next 60 days at 75% pay, next 30 days unpaid, per illness episode (Article 117).
- **End-of-service gratuity:** Half a month's basic salary per year for the first 5 years, a full month's basic salary per year after that (Articles 84-87). Resignation is prorated by tenure.
- **Nitaqat / Saudization:** Companies are banded (Platinum/Green/Yellow/Red) based on the ratio of Saudi nationals employed relative to company size and sector. Higher bands unlock faster government transaction processing (visas, work permits); Red-band companies face restrictions on new visas and iqama renewals. HR must track headcount ratios per entity.

## United Arab Emirates (UAE)

- **Governing law:** Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021.
- **Probation period:** Up to 6 months.
- **Annual leave:** 30 calendar days/year after 1 year of service; 2 days/month accrued during the first year (Article 29).
- **End-of-service gratuity:** 21 days' basic salary per year for the first 5 years, 30 days' basic salary per year after, capped at 2 years' total salary.

## Egypt

- **Governing law:** Labour Law No. 12 of 2003.
- **Probation period:** Up to 3 months.
- **Annual leave:** 21 days/year; 30 days/year once tenure reaches 10 years, or once the employee turns 50 (Article 47).

## Jordan

- **Governing law:** Jordanian Labour Law No. 8 of 1996 (as amended).
- **Probation period:** Up to 3 months.
- **Annual leave:** 14 days/year; 21 days/year once tenure reaches 5 years (Article 61).
